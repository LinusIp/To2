# Legal compliance (S4) — MVP choices and production plan

> Not legal advice. TODO(team): have a lawyer in Uzbekistan review before launch.

## Personal data
- **The law:** Law No. ZRU-547 "On personal data" (2019). Article 27¹, added by ZRU-666 and in force since April 2021, required Uzbek citizens' personal data to be collected and stored on servers physically in Uzbekistan.
- **The 2026 change:** an amendment in force since **27 March 2026** reportedly narrows *mandatory* localisation to **biometric and genetic data** (and telecom subscriber data). Other data may be processed abroad under conditions: international security standards, or a country with adequate protection. Our sources are press reports (yep.uz, daryo.uz), and they differ in the details.
- **Our position: we follow the stricter rule anyway.** We collect **no biometric data**: voice is transcribed and discarded, never stored and never used to identify anyone. The production plan still keeps learner data in an Uzbek data centre. That works under both the old and the new rule.
- TODO(team): a lawyer should confirm the enacted 2026 text, whether a child's learning record needs anything extra, and our registration duties.
- **MVP: data minimisation.** We collect only a *nickname* (real name not required) and *grade*, plus an anonymous random id in an HttpOnly cookie. No phone, email, photo, location or real name.
- Learning data (answers, mastery per skill) is linked only to that anonymous id.
- **Voice is never stored.** Recordings are sent to speech-to-text and discarded; only the text of the question is used for the answer.
- "Hisobni yangidan boshlash" in the profile deletes all of a learner's data.

## Children
- Target users are 10–15 years old. Onboarding states that under-16s use the service with a parent's consent; the privacy page (`/maxfiylik`, in Uzbek) explains what is stored.
- No ads, no third-party trackers or analytics SDKs.
- Content is kid-safe; tutor characters have no weapons or violence.

## Production architecture plan
- Personal data (users, mastery, attempts) moves to a database in a **local Uzbek data centre**; Cloudflare keeps serving the app, static files and caches of non-personal data (lesson audio, generated lesson content).
- VoiceLab (Uzbek provider) processes speech and text. TODO(team): data-processing agreement + where VoiceLab stores data.
- Parental consent flow with a verifiable parent contact for paid accounts.
