# Demo script: Inklyuziv rejim (45 seconds)

**Before the demo**
- Use an Android phone with Chrome. The mic permission must already be granted to the site.
- Sound on, at medium volume.
- Fresh guest, or Sozlamalar → Qulaylik with every preset **off**.
- Run `node scripts/warm-tts.mjs` once, so the lesson lines play instantly.
- Say the voice commands clearly, about 30 cm from the phone. Browser recognition of Uzbek varies by device, so do a dry run first.

| Time | Do | Say (presenter, to the judges) |
|---|---|---|
| **0–8 s** | Splash → tap the **Qulaylik** badge ("To2 — hamma uchun…"). On "Sizga qanday qulay?" tap **Ko'zi ojiz** and **Harakatda qiyinchilik** → **Tayyor, davom etish**. | "Your feedback was inclusion. One tap turns on a mode for blind learners and for learners who can't use their hands. The screen gets bigger and higher-contrast right away." |
| **8–20 s** | Open the lesson (Xarita → current node → *Darsni boshlash*). Put the phone down, hands off. The tutor explains 1/2 + 1/3; when he stops, the board is **read aloud** ("Doskada: ikkidan bir qo'shuv uchdan bir…"). At the question, the options are read: "A javob: …". Say **"javob B"** (or the right letter). | "A voice-only lesson. Everything on the board is also spoken, including fractions and powers, and the learner answers by voice. Nobody touches the screen." |
| **20–30 s** | Say **"to'xta"**, then **"davom et"**. Then **"sekinroq"**. Point at the big caption box and the coloured indicator. *(Optional: Sozlamalar → Eshitishda qiyinchilik beforehand.)* | "Hands-free control: stop, continue, slower. For deaf learners, live captions highlight the word being spoken, a big indicator shows *Ustoz gapiryapti / o'ylayapti*, and the phone vibrates on every new step. Nothing is audio-only." |
| **30–40 s** | Sozlamalar → Qulaylik → tap **Diqqat / disleksiya**. Go back to the lesson. | "For attention difficulties and dyslexia: calm mode with no bouncing or confetti and one sentence at a time, a dyslexia-friendly font with wider spacing, and speech speed from 0.75× to 1.25×." |
| **40–45 s** | On the quiz, say the right option by its number, e.g. **"javob bir"** → it is checked → XP. | "It works in the quiz, the diagnostic and, through one voice-command bus, the town and the games. It's tested with axe-core: 0 serious accessibility errors on 6 screens." |

**If something fails**
- Mic not heard: tap the option instead and keep talking. Every option is also a ≥ 56 px button.
- No voice: the captions carry everything (that is the point of the hearing mode).
- After the demo, turn the presets off again so the next judge starts from the normal app.
